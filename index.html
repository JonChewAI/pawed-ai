Inconsistent AI Pet Portraits

Frequently Asked Questions

Everything You Need to Know About AI Pet Tribute Apps

What is a pawed.ai pet tribute app?
A custom-built AI web application deployed on a personalised subdomain (yourpet.pawed.ai) that generates consistent, realistic images of your pet in any scene, pose, or environment. Each app includes a studio-quality reference library built from your photos using Luma's UNI-1 model - capturing every marking, every tuft of fur, every unique detail.
What photos do I need to provide?
Phone photos are perfect. Share your favourite photos of your pet and we use Luma's UNI-1 model to generate a studio-quality reference library. The more variety (different angles, lighting, poses), the better the reference library. You don't need professional photography - we handle the quality transformation.
How long does it take to build my pet's tribute app?
Each tribute app is custom-built and delivered within 7 days from receiving your photos. This is bespoke engineering work, not a template - your app is built by hand with the same rigour applied to enterprise AI applications.
Do I need any technical knowledge to use it?
None at all. The app is designed for anyone to use. In testing, four out of five users had never touched generative AI and found the experience effortless. You describe a scene, choose a style, and hit generate. Your pet appears in 30 seconds.
What happens when I use all my included generations?
Your included generations (500 or 1,000 depending on tier) are yours forever - they don't expire. When you need more, you can purchase top-up packs starting at $19 for 100 generations. Your tribute app and reference library remain active regardless.
Can I use the generated images for video creation?
Yes. Every image generated through your tribute app is optimised for downstream video generation tools like Kling and Seedance. The consistent reference library means your pet looks the same across every frame - essential for believable video content.
Is this only for pets who have passed away?
Not at all. While pawed.ai was born from a memorial project, tribute apps work beautifully for living pets too. Imagine generating images of your dog in places you plan to visit together, or creating a library of consistent images for content creation. The technology serves any pet owner who wants consistent, realistic AI-generated images of their pet.
